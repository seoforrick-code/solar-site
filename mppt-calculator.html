CHARGE CONTROLLER & FUSE SIZING

What size MPPT controller and PV fuse does your array need?

An undersized controller trips or overheats under full sun; an undersized (or missing) fuse can't protect your wiring if a panel or connector shorts. Both are sized from the same two numbers — your array wattage and system voltage.

How this is calculated

Controller sizing: Max current = Array watts ÷ System voltage. Charge controllers are then sized with a 25% safety margin above that figure (per NEC continuous-load guidance), then rounded up to the nearest common controller size (30A, 40A, 60A, 80A, 100A).

PV fuse sizing: The array-side fuse protects the wiring between panels and controller. It's sized using the panel's short-circuit current (Isc) — typically about 1.25× the panel's rated operating current — with another 1.25× safety factor applied on top, giving a combined multiplier of roughly 1.56× the calculated operating current. This is a general estimate; always check your panel's actual Isc rating on its datasheet, since real-world Isc varies by panel model.

This calculator gives a standard estimate for planning purposes. Final controller and fuse selection should always be checked against your specific panel and controller datasheets, and against your local electrical code (e.g. NEC 690 in the US).
