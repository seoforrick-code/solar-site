About Off-Grid Power Guide

Off-Grid Power Guide is an educational resource designed to make off-grid solar and backup power planning easier to understand.

The site provides practical calculators, guides, and explanations covering solar generators, batteries, solar panels, charge controllers, and other parts of an off-grid power system.

What We Do

Designing an off-grid or backup power system can involve many different numbers: watts, watt-hours, battery capacity, solar production, inverter output, charging current, and more.

Our goal is to turn these concepts into information that is easier to understand and use for preliminary planning.

Solar & Battery Calculators

Interactive tools help estimate daily energy needs, solar array size, and other system-planning values.

Practical Guides

Explanations cover batteries, solar panels, MPPT charge controllers, solar generators, and common off-grid questions.

Planning Information

We explain the assumptions behind calculations so users can understand what an estimate does and does not tell them.

Product Research

Some pages discuss solar generators and other equipment using specifications and publicly available product information.

Our Approach

Off-grid power systems are highly dependent on the equipment, environment, installation, and intended use. For that reason, calculator results on this site are intended as planning estimates, not as universal equipment specifications.

When a calculation depends on manufacturer specifications, electrical ratings, battery chemistry, temperature, solar conditions, or other system-specific factors, those limitations should be taken into account before purchasing or installing equipment.

We aim to make the assumptions behind our calculations clear rather than presenting a single number as guaranteed for every system.

Safety & Limitations

Solar panels, batteries, inverters, charge controllers, wiring, fuses, and other electrical equipment can present serious safety risks when improperly selected or installed.

The calculators and information on this website are intended for general educational and preliminary planning purposes. They do not replace the equipment manufacturer's instructions, applicable electrical codes, or advice from a qualified electrical professional.

Always verify system-specific ratings and installation requirements before purchasing, wiring, or operating equipment.
