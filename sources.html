Sources & References

Off-Grid Power Guide uses technical references, government resources, manufacturer documentation, and established energy information to support its calculators and educational content.

How We Use Sources

Our calculators are designed to provide practical planning estimates. Technical information can vary depending on equipment, installation conditions, location, temperature, and manufacturer specifications.

When a calculation depends on equipment-specific information, users should always verify the result against the documentation supplied by the manufacturer.

Important: Information on this website is provided for educational and planning purposes. It is not a substitute for professional electrical, engineering, or installation advice.

Government & Research Sources

Government

U.S. Department of Energy

The U.S. Department of Energy provides educational and technical information covering solar energy, energy efficiency, renewable energy technologies, and residential energy systems.

Research

National Renewable Energy Laboratory (NREL)

NREL publishes research and technical resources related to solar photovoltaics, renewable energy systems, solar resource assessment, energy technologies, and grid and off-grid applications.

Energy Data

ENERGY STAR

ENERGY STAR provides information about energy-efficient appliances and products. Appliance energy-use information can be useful when estimating daily energy consumption.

Manufacturer Documentation

Manufacturer documentation is particularly important when evaluating real equipment because electrical limits and operating specifications differ between products.

  • Solar panel datasheets for Voc, Vmp, Isc, Imp, power rating, temperature coefficients, and maximum series fuse ratings.
  • Battery manuals for usable capacity, recommended depth of discharge, charging temperature limits, maximum charge and discharge current, and battery management system specifications.
  • Solar generator specifications for battery capacity, continuous AC output, surge capability, solar input limits, charging characteristics, and operating conditions.
  • Charge controller documentation for maximum PV voltage, maximum PV current, charging current, supported battery voltages, and manufacturer installation requirements.
  • Inverter documentation for continuous output, surge capability, DC input requirements, and installation limitations.

For any real installation, the manufacturer's current technical documentation should take priority over generalized estimates presented on this website.

Electrical Standards & Safety

Electrical installations must comply with the codes, standards, permits, and requirements applicable to the installation location.

Depending on the project, relevant requirements may include the National Electrical Code (NEC), local electrical codes, equipment certification requirements, manufacturer instructions, and requirements from the local authority having jurisdiction.

Our calculators should not be interpreted as code-compliance tools or as a replacement for professional electrical design.

Sources Used by Our Calculators

Solar Generator Calculator

Uses basic electrical energy relationships such as watts × hours = watt-hours, combined with clearly identified planning assumptions. Actual appliance consumption can vary substantially.

Solar Panel Calculator

Uses daily energy demand, estimated peak-sun hours, and a planning efficiency factor to estimate an approximate solar array size. Real production varies with weather, orientation, shading, temperature, system losses, and equipment characteristics.

MPPT Calculator

Uses basic PV electrical relationships and planning calculations. Final controller and protection selection must be checked against panel specifications, controller specifications, conductor ampacity, applicable electrical requirements, and manufacturer documentation.

Battery Guide

Battery information is presented as general educational guidance. Actual usable capacity, charging limits, cycle life, temperature limits, and recommended depth of discharge vary by battery model and manufacturer.
