Affiliate Disclosure

Transparency matters to us. This page explains how affiliate links may be used on Off-Grid Power Guide.

Important disclosure:

Some of the links on Off-Grid Power Guide may be affiliate links. This means that if you click an affiliate link and make a qualifying purchase, we may receive a commission from the retailer or affiliate partner.

This does not increase the price you pay for the product.

What are affiliate links?

An affiliate link is a special link that allows a retailer or affiliate network to identify that a visitor arrived at its website from Off-Grid Power Guide.

If the visitor completes a qualifying purchase or other qualifying action, the website may receive a commission.

How affiliate commissions support this website

Running and maintaining a technical website requires time and resources. Affiliate commissions may help support:

  • Website hosting and infrastructure
  • Research and content creation
  • Development and maintenance of calculators
  • Testing and improving website features
  • Future educational resources

Our approach to recommendations

The presence of an affiliate relationship does not mean that every product is automatically recommended.

When we evaluate solar generators, batteries, panels, charge controllers, or other equipment, we aim to consider factors such as:

  • Documented technical specifications
  • Battery capacity and chemistry
  • Continuous and surge power capability
  • Solar charging capabilities
  • Use case and practical limitations
  • Manufacturer documentation
  • Safety considerations
  • Overall suitability for the intended application

Product specifications and prices

Product specifications, prices, availability, warranties, accessories, and retailer policies can change over time.

We therefore recommend checking the manufacturer's current documentation and the retailer's current listing before making a purchase.

Editorial independence

Affiliate relationships do not guarantee that a product will receive a positive recommendation.

Our goal is to provide useful and accurate information that helps readers understand the technical factors involved in choosing off-grid power equipment.
