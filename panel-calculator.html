SOLAR PANEL SIZING

How many watts of solar panels do you actually need?

Sizing your battery is only half the equation — you also need enough panel wattage to fully recharge it each day. That depends on your daily consumption and how many peak sun hours your location gets. Use the calculator below to find your number.

Panel array calculator

Peak sun hours aren't the same as daylight hours — they measure the equivalent hours of full-intensity sunlight your panels receive. Most of the continental US averages 3.5–5.5 peak sun hours; check a solar irradiance map for your specific location.

Required solar array: ~500 W minimum panel capacity

How the calculation works

The formula is: Panel watts = (Daily Wh ÷ Peak sun hours) ÷ System efficiency. System efficiency accounts for inverter conversion loss, cable resistance, panel angle mismatch, and dust or heat derating — typically modeled at around 80% for a well-installed system.

Example: a 1,500 Wh daily load with 4.5 peak sun hours needs roughly 1,500 ÷ 4.5 ÷ 0.8 ≈ 417 W of panels — rounded up to the nearest common panel size, that's a 400–450 W array.
