Safety-Related Questions

Off-grid solar and battery systems involve electricity, stored energy, heat, and potentially high currents. Information on this website should not be treated as a substitute for professional electrical or engineering advice.

If your question involves a real installation, always check the specifications of the equipment you are using and follow applicable safety requirements.
